# Welcome to [My Server]!

> **Sample page:** this is a start page. Write about your server, delete this note, and set `"enabled": true` for this page and `"startPage": "home"` in `config/stats_webui_plus.json`. The ranking then moves to its own menu entry.

[My Server] is a survival server for friends: we build, explore and farm together, and nobody minds a creeper hole now and then.

## Join us

- **Address:** `[play.example.com]`
- **Version:** Minecraft [26.3]
- **Rules:** be kind, no griefing, no cheating; the full rules are in the menu.

## What's on this website

The **ranking** shows who has done the most on the server, the **players** list everyone who has played, and the **advancements** who has achieved what. **Compare** puts two players side by side.
