## What your browser stores

Your choice of language and theme, whether advancements are shown as tiles or as a tree, and whether hidden advancements are hidden, are kept in your browser's local storage; a page's search and tab, for going back to it, until you close the tab. They never leave your device. This website uses no cookies and no trackers.
